Nike and adidas in tennis

My bachelor thesis on how social media builds fan engagement in tennis, comparing Nike and adidas.

Type
Bachelor thesis, ESCP Business School, 2025
Platforms
Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter
Method
Comparative case study, content analysis, expert interview

The question

What makes tennis fans react, share, and stay loyal, and what role does social media play compared with more traditional marketing? I explored two angles:

  • Storytelling and emotional loyalty. How each brand builds stories around athletes, tournaments, and heritage.
  • Platforms and formats. How Instagram, TikTok, and X/Twitter drive engagement differently, and how algorithms and fan behavior shape what works.

What I analyzed

  • Key Nike and adidas tennis campaigns, including athlete partnerships, tournament activations, and product stories
  • Posts, short videos, carousels, and threads on Instagram, TikTok, and X/Twitter
  • Engagement signals such as likes, comments, shares, and views, patterns during major tournaments, and recurring themes in fan comments
  • Industry reports, brand communications, and press coverage, so the findings didn't rely only on what was visible online

How I did it

  1. Comparative case study. I analyzed both brands with the same framework to keep the comparison fair.
  2. Content analysis. I coded content into categories such as athlete role models, tournament stories, heritage, calls to action, community, and entertainment versus performance.
  3. Platform comparison. I looked at how the same message changes by platform: authenticity and trends on TikTok, polished storytelling on Instagram, and real-time conversation on X/Twitter.
  4. Interpretation. I linked the results to how each platform recommends content and to fan psychology, such as identity and the intensity of live events.

Expert interview

I interviewed a former Meta marketing strategist. She stressed the growing power of authentic storytelling, short-form content, and relatability, especially in tennis, where individual players matter more than teams. She saw adidas as a leader here, using collaborations and human-focused campaigns to build community, while Nike's more image-driven approach risks missing the emotional mark. She also explained that TikTok and Instagram reward content that drives real interaction over traditional ads, and that fashion and sport crossovers are helping tennis reach Gen Z.
