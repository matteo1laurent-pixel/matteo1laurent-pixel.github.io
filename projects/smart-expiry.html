Smart Expiry

A mobile app concept to reduce household food waste, designed in an ESCP group project and now in development.

Type
ESCP group project, now in development
Focus
Product design, user needs, sustainability
Now building with
Expo, React Native, TypeScript

The problem

Food often goes to waste for simple reasons: people forget what is in the fridge, miss expiry dates, buy duplicates, or don't know the best way to store food. Living abroad makes it harder, because date formats, labels, and recycling rules change from country to country.

Who it's for

We designed the app around Emily, an international student who moves often and wants to live sustainably but has little time. She needed:

  • A simple view of what she has at home
  • Reminders before food expires
  • Quick recipe ideas using what is already in the fridge
  • Clear recycling guidance for the city she lives in

Our solution

Smart Expiry covers the full cycle, from shopping to disposal:

  1. Account and city. Users choose their city, so recycling guidance matches local rules.
  2. Inventory. Users add groceries by scanning a barcode or typing them in, and the app sorts items by expiry date.
  3. Reminders. Notifications show what to eat first, with quick actions such as "use now" or "remind later."
  4. Storage tips. Each item can show short advice to keep it fresh for longer.
  5. Recipes. When items are close to expiring, the app suggests recipes that use them.
  6. Recycling. The app explains how to dispose of packaging correctly in the user's city, for example Berlin.

We also added an "Article of the Week" section to encourage habits such as meal planning.

What's next

I am now building Smart Expiry as a real mobile app with Expo, aiming for the App Store and Google Play.
