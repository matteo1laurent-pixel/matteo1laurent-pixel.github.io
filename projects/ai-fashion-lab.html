AI Fashion Lab

A project management dossier showing how a virtual try-on service for luxury startup brands could be delivered.

Type
Project management dossier, group project
My role
Team co-coordinator; owner of the training and support costs
Methods
WBS, activity table, precedence diagram, critical path, Gantt chart, cost baseline

The idea

AI Fashion Lab is a virtual try-on and style-matching service for luxury startup brands. It aims to give online shoppers the confidence they usually get in a store, by reducing doubts about fit and style, and to make browsing feel closer to a session with a stylist than a product catalog.

What we delivered

The project was less about building the technology and more about showing how it could be delivered realistically. We treated it as a product rollout with a clear scope, governance, planning, costs, and execution control.

  • Methodology. We explained why our project management approach suits a digital product that needs user testing, iteration, and close delivery control.
  • Planning. We built the full planning package: a work breakdown structure, an activity table, a precedence diagram, the critical path, and a Gantt chart.
  • Cost baseline. We built a structured cost view to support decisions and check feasibility.

My contribution

I was one of the two team coordinators, keeping every part of the dossier aligned with the course requirements and consistent from the work breakdown structure to the Gantt chart.

I also owned the training and support part of the cost baseline. For a tool like this, success depends on adoption, not just launch: brands need to know how to use it, teams need to support it, and users need a smooth experience.
